X Group's Fund Flows on the BSC and ETH Sides

Subjects: X (0xdb34ff92…) / A8 (0xa8d5d100…, Related (direct)) / AF (0xaf24c1b7…, Related (direct)) / W5 (0x5af775f3…111a, Related (direct)) / C8 (0xc8de941e…, Related (inferred, Type S)). Because EVM addresses are the same across chains, their existence, balances, and history on BSC (chainId 56) and Ethereum (chainId 1) were measured directly. Survey date and time: 2026-11-01 (JST). Ethereum was checked using the full history and RPC of the official Blockscout instance, and BSC was checked using a public RPC (the constraints of the history indexer are stated at the end).

Summary of conclusions: On both ETH and BSC, the only 2 that are actually active are AF and C8, and X (self), A8, and W5 were effectively unused on both chains, with zero sending history and zero balances. AF operates as a MetaMask smart account (EIP-7702) on both ETH and BSC, and just before the listing, from 2026-6-14 to 19, it sent a total of 1,228.24 in stablecoins from ETH to a bridge (Mayan) — the timing matches the 1,490 USDT in war funds issued to AF on the 42 Chain side on 6-23, but the amount falls short by about 262 USDT, so the source of the full amount could not be identified. C8 is a veteran wallet that has used the QOM bridge on ETH since 2024, and in 2026-8 it sent 577.135946 USDT to a Hyperlane-based BridgeRouter, which matches, down to the decimal places, the USDT mint (577.14) that C8 received on the 42 Chain side.

4. Flows Inside the Group (ETH and BSC)

Direct transfers among the 5 addresses do not exist on either ETH or BSC (which is also to be expected, since X, A8, and W5 are unused on both chains). There is only 1 indirect point of contact: AF (2026-06-09) and C8 (2026-08-14) both received the same quantity, AICC 17,500, from the same distributor, 0x11Bf2e0d…. It is natural to view this not as a transfer inside the group, but as each of them receiving separately from a common airdrop / distributor. The fact that both AF and C8 use MetaMask-based mechanisms (AF = a DeleGator smart account, C8 = swaps via MetaSwap) also suggests commonality in their operational tools.

6. Limits of Coverage (Honest Notes)

•BSC transaction history could not be obtained. No instance called bsc.blockscout.com exists (404), BscScan API access without a key was blocked as a bot, and Routescan did not support chainId 56. For that reason, for BSC this goes only as far as "existence, nonce, and current balances," and the dates, counterparties, and amounts of AF's 16 sends on BSC are unconfirmed.
•For X, A8, W5, and C8 on BSC, what was confirmed via RPC was "zero balance + zero sends + zero major tokens," which does not 100% rule out a history of receipts only. However, since all balances are zero, no meaningful funds remain.
•The destination chain / recipient of the Mayan transfers, and the final destination within the batch of AF's spam-like tokens (AICC and ETHG), could not be tracked down through free routes.
