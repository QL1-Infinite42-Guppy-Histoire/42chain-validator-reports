What happened next: of A8's DONT acquisitions (total buys 15,044,398.70), 0.30% was sold, 33.2% was put into LP on 42/DONT, 66.5% was transferred to AF, and zero remained. The 100,000 LP tokens (42/DONT) are also currently held by 0x5af775f3bec809b893de59e2f06ef995d280111a (live-confirmed). Of the [42] lots (62,239.99 across the two wallets), A8 used 4,044.48 to buy DONT, 2,363.32 for LP, and sent 18,281.76 to X (self), while AF moved 22,500 to A8, which became the funding source for A8's July sell-down into USDT (3,500 USDT recovered in total). The reason the index net for [42] becomes negative is a known blind spot: native transfers inside contracts — such as unwrap payouts via the router and the [42] leg of LP redemptions — are not recorded in the index, so the receiving side is understated. Accordingly, for "how much [42] remains now", the live balances are taken as authoritative, and the index was used only to identify the direction of flows and the counterparties. The DONT and USDT (ERC-20) ledgers have no such blind spot and match down to the fractions. One more point: that the 1,490 USDT mint to AF was "an issuance from the zero address" is the on-chain fact; "an issuance accompanying a bridge deposit" is an inference from the route.
