Sources of Funds on the ETH Side — Full Tracing to the Ends of the Chains

Subjects: AF (0xaf24c1b76522c0757ded0520e01c6f068e500eb9, Related (direct): related to X) and C8 (0xc8de941efb92925bfd359a7a380399e47bc2b242, Related (inferred, Type S): related to X (inferred)). Removing the 1-hop limit, each fund chain was traced recursively to a natural terminus of one of the following kinds: a labeled exchange wallet / a bridge contract / a token mint / data exhaustion. Sources: the eth.blockscout.com API v2 (full history) plus the same site's legacy API (oldest records and full aggregation using sort=asc). Survey date and time: 2026-11-01 (JST). Times are JST. All amounts are measured values. The deposit and withdrawal totals for huge wallets are based on full aggregation of the legacy API (deduplicated by transaction hash).

Overall conclusion: The chains traced numbered 7 in total.
• AF's main line is 3 hops: AF ← 0xdb3d858d (a payout type that started in 2019) ← 0xdf4b6fb7 ← a cluster of multiple senders from 2017. There are no labels at all, and it ends in data exhaustion amid the crowd of 2017.
• C8's cluster of 2024 is 4 to 5 hops: tracing back the relay of sibling wallets (each wallet falls silent after seeding the next one as its last act), the final sources arrive at market purchases on Uniswap and withdrawals from a MEXC hot wallet. Circulation (loops) within the cluster was also confirmed.
• C8's remaining branches are short: via QOM_BRIDGE (bridge terminus in 1 hop), USDC from a wallet labeled MEXC (exchange terminus in 1 hop), and destination 0xdf959009 (MEXC hot wallet terminus in 1 hop).
• C8's ETH replenishment chain (0x9c19b049) is 2 hops, reaching a disposable relay network of 2018, and that network's receptacle was a contract that began on 2016-07-20 (the very day of the hard fork) with 555,660 ETH.
• AF's portion for month 9 is a Hyperlane bridge terminus in 1 hop. Only the AICC distributor has no fund records remaining and is unresolved (the possibility of a mint / initial allocation).

Limits of the Tracing (Honest Notes)

• The terminus of Chain A is "the multiple-sender cluster of 2017": each wallet at hop3 began by receiving from many EOAs, and it cannot be decomposed into a single origin. No traces of passage through a mixer such as Tornado Cash were confirmed on this chain (to be precise, none appeared).
• 0x9c19b049 (80 ten-thousand items) and 0x30741289 (13.6 ten-thousand items) were excluded from full aggregation, and were evaluated using counter values plus period samples. Unlike 0xdb3d858d, the lifetime deposit and withdrawal totals for these 2 have not been calculated.
• The lifetime deposit and withdrawal totals for 0xdf4b6fb7 have also not been calculated (transactions 70,637 items). What is known is only the very earliest flows (receipt of 2,428 ETH / 800 ETH to hop1).
• The QOM_BRIDGE / Hyperlane termini mean a terminus "on the ETH side," and identifying the sender on the departure chain side (QL1 / other chains) is outside the scope of this survey.
• Because AF is an EIP-7702 smart account, retrieving the list of its internal transfers timed out, and matching by ETH lot unit is unfinished (however, the fact that its direct ETH inflows are only the 2 from hop1 is established by the full history of its normal transactions).
