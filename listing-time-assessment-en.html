Assessment of the Listing Time: Was 14:00 on 6/30 the Official Listing?

Subject: The 42/USDT pool and the 42/DONT pool of 42 Chain. The basis is the measurement of all transaction data (EVM 86,106 items). The conclusion is at the end of this page.

Conclusion: I judge that there is no doubt. The view that 6/30 14:00 (JST) is the official listing strongly matches the on-chain evidence. 2 pools in which trading had been almost nonexistent for many weeks simultaneously transition, starting at this time, to trading with real demand, and wallets that are considered to be related parties carry out advance buying aligned just before it (28–41 minutes before). This is a movement that can be explained naturally only if there was a “scheduled listing time” and that time was 14 o'clock.

Evidence 3: The Existence of Advance Buying Itself Becomes Proof of a “Scheduled Time”

Advance buying cannot be established unless one knows “from what time it starts.” The related wallets of X start moving simultaneously at 28 minutes before in DONT and 41 minutes before in USDT, at timings that would not align by coincidence. The prices of both pools had also already been pushed up before listing ([42] was 0.01 → 0.0756 USDT, and DONT was +36% in [42] terms). This is a movement in which the related parties, knowing the scheduled time, positioned themselves in alignment with that time.

Honest Reservations

• What the chain data proves is only up to the point that “the market substantially started at 14 on 6/30” and the nature of it being “official” is based on off-chain information, namely an announcement by the operating side. The two match, but the word “official” does not emerge from the data alone.
• In 42/USDT, the advance buying was large, and the trading volume in the 13 hour period (43 trades) exceeded that in the 14 hour period (37 trades). “The release was at 14 o'clock, but the related parties had been buying from before that” is the accurate description.
• All of the times are based on block timestamps (converted to JST). The clock of the chain shows an almost constant block interval throughout the whole period, and no major problem is seen in the reliability of the times.
