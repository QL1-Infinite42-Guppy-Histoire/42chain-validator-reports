1. Before the Listing (2026-05-25 – 06-30 13:00)

The pair was created on 2026-05-25 at 13:58 (JST). The initial LP added that same day by the operator-type wallet and a FoxxOne-related wallet (1,000 + 800 LP) was transferred to the initial test wallet and fully redeemed within the day, leaving the pool empty. On 2026-05-27 at 09:22, that wallet re-seeded with 1 DONT + 1 42 and made 13 tiny test trades over about 55 minutes (almost all between less than 1 DONT and 50 DONT). From that afternoon through 06-06, LP adds of nearly identical size, approx. 70,573.8–70,583.9 LP, were made by 16 wallets. Most of them were the validators themselves or related wallets, in the form of an equal-size distribution. In late June (06-23/24/27), two non-validator wallets each made just one tiny round-trip test trade (0.002–0.005 42); there is no sign of continuous trading such as price maintenance (these were test trades of negligible size relative to the pool).

6. Comparing the Creation Window and the Listing Window

The creation window (2026-05-25)was a "creation ceremony" involving only the operator-type wallet and one funded related wallet; the LP they added was fully withdrawn within hours, leaving the pool empty. After that, real activity consisted only of test trades by a single wallet on 2026-05-27 and the equal-size LP distribution (16 wallets, most of them validator-related), and trading was almost dormant throughout June.
The listing window (2026-06-30 14:00–)was in stark contrast: it began with pre-positioning buys by an X-related wallet 28 minutes before the listing, an outsider's first trade came in 3 seconds after the listing, and 29 swaps from 11 wallets followed within 3 hours. There were 28 buys against only 1 sell, and the day's peak (19 swaps in the 14:00 hour) coincides exactly with the listing hour. It was a case of "real demand arriving for the first time, at the announced time, in a pool that had been prepared" — showing that the creation and the listing were entirely separate events.
