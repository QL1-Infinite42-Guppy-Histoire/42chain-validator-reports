Conclusion: What the X Group Holds Now, and Where It Came From

Subject: validator X itself, plus 3 related wallets (0xa8d5… = A8, 0xaf24… = AF, 0x5af7… = W5), plus 1 inferred related wallet (0xc8de… = C8). Confirmed against actual balances as of 2026-10-31.

In short: X's own wallet is almost empty, and all of the assets sit in the related wallets. The real vault is W5, which holds the 42/DONT pool LP 100,000 (8.13% of the pool), the USDT/42 pool LP 1,000 (7.88% of the pool), DONT spot holdings of 500 ten-thousand, and QOM of about 5,729 hundred-million, along with 6 small LPs in FISH-related pools and spot tokens such as CAW of about 410 hundred-million and OSAK of about 18.7 hundred-million. AF, the 1 other related wallet, still holds QOM of about 58.6 hundred-million, CAW of about 123.6 hundred-million, and more. All of this originated from trades made just before the listing (on the 30th day of the 6th month), when a war chest of 1,490 USDT was used to stock up on [42] and DONT cheaply. The rewards received in FISH were mostly turned into liquidity for the FISH/42 pool, and ultimately consolidated into the shared custody contract used by many people.

Illustrated: How the Money Flowed and Took Its Current Shape

① The war chest arrives (the 23rd day of the 6th month)
1,490 USDT is issued to AF's wallet (it appears to be money that came in via a bridge). Funding from X itself is almost zero.
▼ Split it up
② Sharing it out (the 25th day of the 6th month)
AF passes 740 USDT to A8 and keeps 750 USDT itself, setting up a 2-wallet buying arrangement.
▼ Listing day (the 30th day of the 6th month)
③ Stocking up just before the listing (13:18 to 13:34)
A8 and AF buy [42] in the 42/USDT pool (the price rises from 0.01 to 0.076), and then A8 uses that [42] to buy DONT of 1,201 ten-thousand (DONT also rises +36% before the listing).
▼ Putting what they bought to work
④ Turning it into positions (the 30th day of the 6th month to the 8th month)
- Of the DONT bought, 500 ten-thousand goes into the 42/DONT pool as liquidity (receiving 108,424 LP tokens)
- The USDT is recovered by selling [42] and reused to buy again in rotation; part of it also goes into liquidity for the USDT/42 pool
- DONT spot holdings of 1,000 ten-thousand move to AF and circulate within the group
▼ Consolidation (from the 5th day of the 8th month)
⑤ Consolidation into the vault, W5
The 42/DONT LP of 100,000 and the USDT/42 LP of 4,200 are delivered from AF to W5 minutes apart on the same day (W5 removes 3,000 of the USDT/42 LP and keeps 1,000). The FISH-related LPs are sent to the shared contract, and DONT spot holdings of 500 ten-thousand and QOM of about 5,729 hundred-million remain in W5.

Note: an LP token is like a certificate showing you deposited money into a pool. The share you hold is your share of that pool. You can burn it at any time to turn it back into money.
